How to Bulk Edit Shopify Product Prices (Without CSV Export)

Changing prices on 200 products shouldn't take 3 hours and an Excel file. Here's a faster way that doesn't involve exporting, editing, and reimporting anything.

The problem with Shopify's native bulk editor

Shopify's built-in bulk editor lets you select multiple products and make changes — but it's limited. You can't filter products easily, you can't apply percentage rules across a selection, and you certainly can't preview what you're about to change before committing.

The alternative most merchants resort to is the CSV export workflow: export your catalog, open it in Excel or Google Sheets, apply formulas, fix the formatting, and reimport. If anything goes wrong during the import — a wrong column header, a missing variant SKU — Shopify either silently ignores the row or throws a generic error.

For a one-off change on 10 products, it's manageable. For 500 products during a flash sale, it's a risk.

"I need to apply a 15% discount to my whole catalog for a weekend promo. In Shopify I have to export, edit in Excel, reimport — and pray the format is right." — common complaint from Shopify merchants

What inline bulk editing looks like

A proper bulk editor loads your entire product catalog in a table — title, price, compare-at price, SKU, tags, vendor, status — all in one view. You click a cell and edit it directly. No exports. No imports. No format to get right.

Editing a single product

Click the price cell, type the new value, move on. The change is staged — nothing is sent to Shopify until you explicitly push it. This means you can edit 50 products and then review all the changes before anything goes live.

Bulk price rules across multiple products

Select multiple rows, open the bulk price action, and apply a rule: set a fixed price, apply a percentage decrease, round to the nearest .99. Every selected product gets updated in your staging view instantly.

Reviewing the diff before saving

Before pushing to Shopify, you see exactly what's changing: old price → new price, for every product. If you made a mistake — set something to €0.50 instead of €5.00 — you catch it here, not after 200 orders have been placed.

Step by step: bulk edit prices on Shopify without CSV

  1. Connect your store with one-click OAuth — no credentials stored
  2. Your catalog loads instantly in a spreadsheet-style table
  3. Filter by collection, tag, vendor, or status to narrow your selection
  4. Select the products you want to change
  5. Apply a bulk price rule (fixed, percentage, or per-product)
  6. Review the full diff — every change shown before it goes live
  7. Push to Shopify in one click

When this approach is faster than CSV

The CSV workflow makes sense if you're migrating a catalog or need to change fields that aren't exposed in a UI. For pricing, discounts, tags, status, and SEO — inline editing is always faster.

The math is simple: exporting takes 30 seconds, opening and editing in Excel takes 10-30 minutes depending on catalog size, reimporting takes another minute, and verifying the import worked correctly takes however long it takes to spot the errors. A 200-product price update done inline takes under 5 minutes.

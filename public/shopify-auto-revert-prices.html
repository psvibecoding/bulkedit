How to Auto-Revert Shopify Prices After a Sale (Set It and Forget It)

Running a weekend sale means changing prices twice: once to go on sale, once to revert. Most merchants only remember the first part. Here's how to automate both.

The problem: forgetting to revert sale prices

You run a flash sale. You drop prices on 150 products Friday evening. The weekend goes well. Monday comes, you're busy with orders, emails, support — and the sale prices are still live. By Tuesday afternoon you've sold another 40 orders at the wrong price.

This isn't a rare edge case. It happens to merchants regularly, especially during high-traffic events like BFCM, summer sales, or seasonal promos where the original task of reverting prices gets buried under everything else that follows a successful weekend.

"Forgot to revert my sale prices after BFCM. Sold at discount for 3 days before I noticed. Lost a lot of margin on ~180 orders before I caught it." — Shopify merchant

What auto-revert means

Auto-revert means you set the sale price and the revert price at the same time, before the sale starts. The system schedules both changes: the discount goes live at the time you specify, and the original prices are restored automatically at the end time you set — with no action required from you.

The original prices are captured at the moment you create the schedule, so there's no risk of reverting to the wrong value.

How to set up auto-revert on Shopify

Shopify doesn't have native auto-revert. The platform lets you change prices manually or via CSV, but it has no built-in mechanism to schedule a price to revert to its previous value at a specific time.

The workflow using Lederly:

  1. Load your catalog and make the sale price changes (inline, no CSV)
  2. Instead of pushing directly, click Schedule
  3. Set the go-live date and time (e.g. Friday 00:00)
  4. Enable Auto-revert and set the end time (e.g. Sunday 23:59)
  5. Confirm — both changes are now scheduled

From that point, nothing else is needed. Your prices go on sale automatically and revert automatically. You get an email confirmation when each change executes.

What gets reverted — and what doesn't

Auto-revert captures the exact values at the time you create the schedule: price, compare-at price, or any other field you changed. When the revert executes, those original values are restored product by product.

If a product's price was manually changed between the sale start and the revert time, the revert will still restore to the value captured at schedule creation — not to whatever was set in between. This is intentional: you always know exactly what will be reverted.

Which fields can be reverted?

  • Price and compare-at price (most common)
  • Product status (ACTIVE / DRAFT) — useful for limited drops
  • Tags — add a "sale" tag during the promo, remove it automatically
  • Vendor, title, and other product fields

Common use cases

Black Friday / Cyber Monday

Schedule discounts across your entire catalog for the BFCM weekend. Set auto-revert for Monday midnight. No alarm. No Monday morning panic.

Flash sales

24-hour or 48-hour promotions that need to start and end precisely. Set both at the same time before the campaign launches.

Seasonal pricing

Summer pricing that reverts in September. Holiday bundles that go back to individual pricing in January. Any time-bounded change to your catalog.

Limited product drops

Schedule a product to go from DRAFT to ACTIVE at launch time, and back to DRAFT if the drop ends at a specific time — all without touching Shopify manually.

What happens if the revert fails?

Lederly sends an email notification for every scheduled change that executes. If a revert encounters an error on a specific product — for example, if the product was deleted between the schedule creation and execution — you're notified immediately with the details.

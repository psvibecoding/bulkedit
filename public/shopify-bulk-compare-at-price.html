How to Bulk Update Compare At Price on Shopify (Show the Strikethrough)

The "Compare At" price is what creates the strikethrough effect on your product pages — showing customers what they're saving. Setting it on hundreds of products one by one isn't realistic. Here's how to do it in bulk.

What is the Compare At price in Shopify?

Every Shopify product variant has two price fields: Price (the actual selling price) and Compare At Price (the "was" price shown with a strikethrough). When the Compare At price is higher than the Price, Shopify displays the original as crossed out and calculates a "% off" badge automatically in most themes.

It's one of the most effective conversion levers available — showing a discount visually, without requiring a discount code. But it only works if you actually set both fields, and setting them across a large catalog product-by-product is impractical.

How to bulk update Compare At price in Lederly

Lederly displays both the Price and Compare At price columns side by side in the product table. You can edit them inline or apply bulk rules.

Setting Compare At to current price, then discounting

This is the most common BFCM workflow:

  1. Load your catalog — both price columns are visible
  2. Select the products you want to put on sale
  3. Set Compare At = current Price (captures the original as the "was" price)
  4. Apply a percentage reduction to Price (the actual sale price)
  5. Review the diff — every product shows old price / new price / new compare-at
  6. Push or schedule with auto-revert

Clearing Compare At after a sale

When auto-revert runs, Lederly restores both the Price and the Compare At to their original values. The strikethrough disappears automatically when the sale ends — no manual cleanup.

If you want to clear Compare At without touching prices, select the products and set Compare At to empty. The strikethrough disappears and the selling price is unchanged.

Shopify only shows the strikethrough when Compare At is strictly greater than Price. If you accidentally set them equal, or if a rounding rule makes Price > Compare At, the strikethrough won't appear. Lederly flags these cases in the diff before you push.

Bulk rules for Compare At price

Set Compare At as a fixed markup over Price

Useful for stores that always show an original retail price. Set Compare At = Price × 1.3 (or whatever your standard markup is) across a selection. Every product's original price is calculated from the current price, not entered manually.

Set Price as a percentage of Compare At

Inverse approach: you already have Compare At set (the original retail price), and you want to calculate the sale price. Set Price = Compare At × 0.70 for 30% off. The "was" price stays fixed; only the sale price changes.

Common mistakes with Compare At price

Forgetting to clear it after a sale

The most common issue. After a promotion ends, the Compare At stays set and your products permanently look like they're on sale — even at full price. This erodes trust over time and may violate advertising standards in some markets. Auto-revert eliminates this risk entirely.

Setting it too high

A Compare At price that's 3× the actual price looks manipulated. Customers recognize inflated "original" prices and discount the credibility of the discount. Keep Compare At to your actual regular selling price.

Not updating it when base prices change

If your regular price increases but Compare At stays at the old level, the strikethrough math becomes inaccurate. Review Compare At whenever you do a price update.

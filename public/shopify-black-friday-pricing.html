Shopify Black Friday Pricing: How to Prepare, Schedule and Revert Prices

BFCM is the highest-stakes week in e-commerce. Manually pushing prices at midnight and hoping someone remembers to revert them on Monday is not a strategy. Here's how to set it up once and let it run.

The two biggest BFCM pricing mistakes

1. Prices go live late

Your email campaign says the sale starts at midnight. Your countdown timer agrees. But the actual Shopify prices don't update until you log in and manually push them — which means 9am, or when you wake up, or after someone complains on social media. The disconnect between your marketing and your actual store prices is a trust problem.

2. Prices don't revert

The other common failure: BFCM prices stay in place for days after the sale ends. Customers buy at sale price when they shouldn't. Your margins take a hit. Someone has to notice, log in, and fix it manually — and by then the damage is done.

Both problems have the same root cause: relying on manual intervention during the busiest and most distracted time of the year.

How to set up BFCM pricing the right way

Step 1 — Prepare your prices in advance

At least 48 hours before BFCM, load your Shopify catalog in Lederly and set your sale prices. Use the bulk rules to apply percentage discounts across product categories — for example, 30% off all products tagged outerwear, 20% off everything else.

Review the resulting prices in the diff table before staging anything. Catch any products where the discount produces an unexpected or unacceptably low price.

Step 2 — Schedule the go-live

Instead of pushing immediately, click Schedule and set the go-live to exactly when your sale starts — midnight Thursday, 9am Friday, or whatever your campaign specifies. The changes execute automatically at that time whether you're awake or not.

Step 3 — Set the auto-revert

When scheduling, set the auto-revert time to Monday midnight (or whenever your sale ends). Lederly stores your original prices alongside the scheduled changes and restores them automatically when the revert time hits.

You get email confirmations when both the go-live and the revert execute. No manual checking required.

The auto-revert uses the prices that were live before you staged the schedule — not a hardcoded value you have to enter. So even if prices changed in the weeks leading up to BFCM, the revert goes back to the right number.

BFCM pricing strategy options

Flat percentage off sitewide

The simplest approach: one discount rate applied to every product. Easy to communicate ("20% off everything"), easy to execute in bulk. Leaves margin on the table for high-demand products but minimizes decision fatigue.

Tiered discounts by category

Higher discounts on slower-moving inventory, lower discounts on best-sellers. Requires more setup (separate bulk rules per category tag) but protects margin where demand is strong.

Staggered sale windows

Black Friday pricing from midnight to midnight, then a second "Cyber Monday" pricing starting Saturday. Two separate schedules — one to switch prices at the boundary, one to revert after Cyber Monday. Each schedule is independent and has its own revert.

What about Shopify discount codes?

Discount codes are a parallel strategy, not a replacement. A discount code requires the customer to find and enter the code — there's friction, and customers who don't know the code see full prices. For a sitewide BFCM sale where your marketing is driving to a product page (not a checkout), changing the actual listed price is essential for conversion.

Use discount codes for email-exclusive deals. Use price changes for the storefront-visible sale price.

BFCM pricing checklist

  • Confirm sale prices and discounts at least 48h before BFCM
  • Set schedule go-live to match your campaign exactly (with timezone)
  • Set auto-revert to the minute your sale ends
  • Check that original prices are correctly captured in the schedule
  • Verify email notifications are enabled for schedule confirmation
  • Test on one product a week before to confirm the scheduling flow works

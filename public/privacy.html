Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Lederly is a bulk product editing tool for Shopify. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What data we collect

  • Shopify access token — used only during your session to read and write products on your behalf. Stored in your browser's sessionStorage only. Never saved on our servers.
  • Store domain — used to identify your store for plan tracking and usage limits.
  • Store email — fetched from Shopify on first connection to send a welcome email. Not stored permanently.
  • Notification email — if provided when creating a schedule, used to send a confirmation. Stored with the schedule record.
  • Waitlist email — stored to contact you about plan availability if you join the waitlist.
  • Usage counts — push count and schedule count per store per month, for plan limit enforcement. No product data is stored.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store your product data, prices, or catalog content.
  • We do not store your Shopify access token server-side.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Third-party services

  • Shopify — product data is read from and written to your store via the Shopify Admin API.
  • Resend — used to send transactional emails. Your email address is shared with Resend for delivery.
  • Railway — our hosting provider (EU region).

Data retention

Schedule records are automatically deleted 30 days after execution. Usage counts reset monthly. You can request deletion at any time.

Security

Shopify tokens used for scheduled operations are encrypted at rest with AES-256-CBC. All communication is over HTTPS. Rate limiting and input validation are applied on all endpoints.

Questions? Email privacy@lederly.com or reply to any email we've sent you.

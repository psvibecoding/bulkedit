Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Lederly ("we", "our", "the service") is a web-based tool that lets Shopify store owners edit their products in bulk. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

What we collect

We collect as little data as possible:

  • Store domain — entered by you to connect your store. Never stored after your session ends.
  • Shopify access token — obtained via Shopify OAuth. Stored only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session. Never written to our servers, logs, or databases during normal use.
  • Scheduled changes — if you use the Schedule feature, your Shopify access token is temporarily encrypted with AES-256 and stored on our server until the schedule executes. It is deleted immediately after execution.
  • Contact messages — if you send us a message via the contact form, we receive your name, email and message text. We do not store these beyond our email inbox.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account registration, passwords, or user profiles
  • No product data stored on our servers (data flows directly between your browser and Shopify)
  • No tracking cookies or advertising pixels
  • No analytics or third-party tracking scripts

Shopify OAuth

When you connect your store, you are redirected to Shopify's own login page. Lederly never sees your Shopify password. The OAuth access token returned by Shopify is used only to make API calls on your behalf during your session.

The app will appear under Apps in your Shopify Admin. You can revoke access at any time from there, which immediately invalidates the token.

Scheduled changes

When you create a scheduled edit, your Shopify access token is encrypted using AES-256-CBC and stored in a secure file on our server. It is decrypted only at execution time, then immediately deleted. If you cancel a schedule, the encrypted token is deleted at that point.

Email notifications

If you have scheduled changes, we may send a confirmation email to the email address associated with your Shopify store when changes go live. This is sent via Brevo (brevo.com) and we do not store your email address beyond what is contained in the schedule record.

Infrastructure

Lederly is hosted on Railway (railway.app). Standard server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) may be retained by Railway for security and operational purposes, subject to their own privacy policy.

Your rights

Since we do not store personal data beyond the transient cases described above, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have a concern, contact us via the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
